    04 About

    A little more context.

    I graduated from the University of Maryland, College Park in May 2026 with a B.S. in Computer Science (machine learning concentration) and a minor in Business, as a Presidential Scholar. Coursework took me through algorithms, operating systems, security, parallel computing, and computer vision.

    Outside class I interned on a network engineering team, built a handful of projects end to end, and spent four summers teaching Python and Java to students of all ages. Teaching made me good at explaining things, and at reading code I didn’t write. Before college I led my high school’s FIRST Robotics team and founded its CyberPatriot team.

    I pick up new languages and frameworks quickly, and I care about the unglamorous parts too: tests, edge cases, and the last 10% that makes something feel finished.
